Every visit ends with a written report

Within 24 hours of each visit, your facility manager gets a report for every unit we touched: what we checked, the readings we took, photos, and anything that needs attention before it becomes a breakdown.

  • Readings logged per unit, so a compressor drifting out of range is caught months early
  • Before and after photos of coils, filters and drain pans
  • Parts recommendations with prices. Nothing is replaced without your approval
  • A full service history for audits, insurers and building handovers

How a contract starts

From first call to first visit usually takes about a week.

  1. Free site survey

    An engineer walks the plant room, roof and floors, and records the condition of every unit.

  2. Equipment register

    Each unit gets a tag and an ID, so every visit, reading and repair is tracked against it.

  3. Scope and fixed price

    You receive a written scope, visit calendar and price. Nothing starts until you approve it.

  4. Visits begin

    Scheduled maintenance, a report after each visit, and the 24/7 line active from day one.

Questions facility managers ask us

Something else on your mind? Call or WhatsApp and speak to an engineer directly.

Do you service homes or villas?

No. We only work on commercial buildings, so our team, tools and spare parts are set up for central plant, large ducted systems and multi-floor sites.

Can you take over from our current maintenance contractor?

Yes. We start with a full survey and build an equipment register, so nothing is missed in the handover. We can begin mid-contract or when your current contract ends.

Can you work at night or on weekends?

Yes. For malls, offices and hotels we regularly schedule work outside trading or office hours so occupants aren't disturbed.

How is a maintenance contract priced?

By the number and type of units, how often they're visited, and the level of breakdown and parts cover you choose. The site survey is free and you get a fixed yearly price.

Which brands and systems do you work on?

All major makes, including Carrier, Trane, York, Daikin, Mitsubishi Electric, LG, Samsung, Gree, Midea and Zamil, across chillers, AHUs, FCUs, VRF, package and ducted split units.
